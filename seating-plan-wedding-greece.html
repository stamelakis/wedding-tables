Timeline

What opens when, and how long it lasts

The date of your wedding sets everything. You bought nothing, so there is no waiting period before you start.

  • As soon as you sign upYour link arrives in your email. If you do not open it within 30 days, the plan is deleted.
  • From day oneGuests, invitations, groups, and who sits where at the tables that are already set.
  • 30 days beforeThe room opens too: add, move and remove tables, up to 8 guest tables. If you sign up within these 30 days, the plan opens complete straight away.

Questions

What people ask us most

Is it really free?

Yes. You give no card, and the free plan never turns into a paid one by itself. You pay only if you ask us for more than 8 guest tables.

How many guests fit on 8 tables?

It depends on the seats at each table: with ten seats that is eighty guests, with twelve it is ninety-six. One main table, such as the couple's, does not count towards the 8.

What if we need more tables?

Write to us and, once the 25 € (VAT included) is paid, we unlock the same plan: everything you have built stays as it is, and the table limit goes. You get a receipt.

Can we pay by card?

Not at the moment. The unlock is paid by bank transfer, and we send you the details by email when you ask for it. The free plan needs no payment at all.

Is everything in English?

The planner, the lists, the PDF floor plan and our emails to you are in English. You can switch the planner to Greek at any time from its menu, for example to print a copy for the venue's staff. Your guests' names stay exactly as you type them.

Our venue already uses TakeaSeat. Do we need this?

No. Ask the venue for your link: the room is already drawn, there is no table limit and you pay nothing. If you are not sure whether your venue works with us, ask us.

What happens if we do not use it?

If you do not open the link we sent you within 30 days, the plan is deleted. If later you do not open it for 6 months, we send you an email; if you do not open it within 14 days of that, it is deleted. Opening it once is enough to keep it.

What happens to the plan after the wedding?

From the next day nobody can change it: it stays view-only, you can still download the PDFs, and your guests' notes are erased. One year after the wedding it is deleted for good, together with your guests' names.

Where is our data kept?

On a server in Germany, inside the European Union, with an encrypted backup every night. There are no cookies and no ads. The Privacy Policy has the details, in English.

We are a wedding planner based in Greece. How do we start?

Write to us and we set up your console with you. The first year is free, with no limit on events; after that it is 349 € per year plus VAT, with an invoice and no automatic charge. The free year is given once per Greek tax number (ΑΦΜ).
